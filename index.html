More than a surface

Building Envelope | Space | Performance

The building envelope is more than a surface.

It is the interface between architecture, environment and human space — mediating climate, light, energy, material, comfort and the relationship between inside and outside.

ENVLO approaches the envelope as an integrated architectural and environmental system, bringing together design, technical development and performance.

Designing better envelopes for a better built environment

Purpose

ENVLO seeks to develop building envelopes that use material intelligently, respond to climate, improve environmental performance and consider their impact throughout the building lifecycle.

Our approach brings together

  • Performance
  • Material efficiency
  • Durability
  • Adaptability
  • Energy
  • Carbon
  • Lifecycle

03

About ENVLO

Practice

ENVLO is a specialist building-envelope and façade consultancy connecting Cairo and Berlin.

Integration

We integrate architectural thinking, façade design and performance-based analysis to develop building envelopes responsive to climate, material, context and use.

Approach

Our approach considers the building envelope not as an isolated skin, but as an integrated architectural and environmental system.

Aim

Our aim is to develop building envelopes that balance architectural intent, technical performance, material responsibility and long-term value.
